DUSTFALL — Meridian Relay: a round-based tactical FPS in three.js. Two teams of five fight over a dusk desert relay station: the Vanguard attack, the Wardens defend. Plant the device at Relay or Silo, or wipe the other team. First to 5 rounds takes the match.

Five weapons (pistol, carbine, SMG, shotgun, sniper) and three grenades (frag, smoke, flash) with a round economy — win $3,250, lose $1,900 plus a loss streak bonus, kills pay $300. Nine bots with real AI: waypoint routing, line-of-sight vision, hearing, bursts with reaction time, site holds, rotations to a planted bomb, planting and defusing. Hit detection separates head, torso and legs; spread grows with movement, airtime and sustained fire; recoil kicks the aim. Fixed 60 Hz simulation apart from rendering, pooled effects, procedural WebAudio — no assets, no network, runs offline in a desktop browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
